Mirage SLA V0¶

This page covers the development integration. Use a checkout containing src/ctrlp/environments/mirage/ and the authored experiments/mirage/ controls. Older source mirrors and the frozen Paper 1 snapshot do not provide this environment. These examples require the optional integration and are reported separately by the documentation checker when its source is unavailable.

mirage/sla-v0 is a continuous-power, single-layer laser resin-curing environment, ported from Mirage-Optics/mirage-sla-rl at 7aad5c014e8d72295344a404b528832dfe862eec.

The model combines a Gaussian laser, QSSA photopolymerisation with oxygen inhibition, and explicit finite-volume oxygen diffusion with Neumann boundaries. It does not model depth, attenuation through the layer, multilayer history, radical diffusion, surface oxygen resupply or optical ray bending. It is a process-control surrogate, not a validated printer model.

Task and interfaces¶

The physical grid is 100x100 cells at 10 micrometres per cell. The fixed serpentine raster scans the central 80x80 window, one position per millisecond. An episode contains all 6400 physical transitions. The agent chooses power, not scan direction or speed.

  • Action: float32 Box(0, 1, (1,)), clipped as upstream; power is action times P_max (default 50 mW).

  • Observation: flattened 11x11 patches of X/X_gel, oxygen/initial oxygen and target, followed by optional scan progress. Original: 364 values; v4: 363. Conversion observations can exceed one and have upper bound 1/X_gel.

  • Natural completion terminates; an earlier vec.max_steps truncates. The scalar environment requires reset after completion. The SB3 VecEnv preserves terminal observations and metrics before resetting the finished lane.

  • reset(seed=...) repeats the target sequence. The first reset uses that upstream JAX key directly; subsequent unseeded resets advance independent keys. Validation and test modes fold distinct keys into legacy random-target seeds. reset(options={"target": mask}) accepts a nonempty binary 100x100 mask.

  • snapshot() explicitly copies conversion, oxygen, target, step and energy. snapshot(terminal=True) accesses the most recent terminal state, including after vector autoreset. render() returns an offscreen RGB image.

The original preset uses the reference square with a hole and linear cure-match reward. The v4 preset uses random targets, removes progress and sets outside weight -4, oxygen shaping 0.3 and gel-step width 0.1. Reward is the upstream potential difference, including its original discount behavior; it is not changed to a new policy-invariant shaping formula.

info.score and info.iou measure binary cure IoU with strict X > X_gel. cured_fraction divides cured target cells by target cells; overcure_fraction divides cured outside cells by all outside cells, over the whole 100x100 grid. energy_j accumulates applied power times dt. These metrics remain distinct from accumulated reward. An empty union has IoU 1; an empty target has cured fraction 1. Supplied empty target overrides are rejected.

Run through Zoo¶

Use the WSL/Linux Python and the existing optional JAX runtime for that host. CPU JAX suffices for tests; use the host’s GPU profile for GPU execution. No runtime import of a sibling Mirage checkout is needed. Rendering also needs Matplotlib; it uses the offscreen Agg canvas.

From the Zoo root:

PYTHONPATH=src .venv-wsl/bin/python ctrlp.py experiments/mirage/sla-v0/runs/original/ppo/smoke/run.yml --mode resolve
PYTHONPATH=src .venv-wsl/bin/python ctrlp.py experiments/mirage/sla-v0/runs/original/ppo/smoke/run.yml --mode check --envs 1

The sibling runs/v4/ppo/smoke/run.yml selects the random-target variant. Both controls request one bounded 1024-transition PPO rollout, with training-time evaluation disabled. They are software smoke controls, not research studies. Their normal vector settings use eight batched environments, no normalization, stack 1 and no shorter time limit. SB3 PPO runs on CPU; JAX physics uses its configured backend. Host-policy transfers mean this path is not the upstream fully fused PPO throughput benchmark. Native Zoo PPO remains discrete-only.

Run deterministic target-following 20/25/30 mW baselines against an explicit control without training:

PYTHONPATH=src .venv-wsl/bin/python -m ctrlp.environments.mirage.sla.baseline experiments/mirage/sla-v0/runs/original/ppo/smoke/run.yml --output .local/mirage-original-baselines.json

Use the v4 control and --seeds 10000 10001 10002 for reproducible random cases. Seeds 10000 through 10015 are upstream’s historical evaluation cases, not new held-out research cases. The report records full-scan metrics and separates compilation from execution. Upstream reported that a tuned constant-power scan outperformed its policy; establish headroom against a tuned baseline before proposing research training.

Shape accuracy pilot¶

The shape_accuracy task retains the same raster, physics and observations, including progress. Only intensity is controlled. Its reward is the change in smooth correct cure minus outside cure, divided by target area and scaled by 100. Oxygen shaping is disabled, outside weight is -1 and PPO gamma is 1. Thus the undiscounted return measures final geometric accuracy; energy remains a diagnostic rather than a second objective. The original/v4 rewards remain available unchanged.

Training generates fresh random masks and rejects every held-out mask hash. assets/shape-bank-v1.json freezes 32 validation masks and 64 test masks: 32 unseen random instances, and eight each of letters, polygons, rings and narrow gaps. The latter families use Lite geometry construction. Validation and test have distinct IDs and hashes, independent of PPO seed. Rebuild with scripts/build_mirage_shape_bank.py; a changed bank requires a new version. This is a small exploratory generalization test, not coverage of all shapes.

The maintained native CLI controls are:

PYTHONPATH=src .venv-wsl/bin/python ctrlp.py experiments/mirage/sla-v0/runs/shape_accuracy/ppo/calibration/run.yml --mode rollout
PYTHONPATH=src .venv-wsl/bin/python ctrlp.py experiments/mirage/sla-v0/runs/shape_accuracy/ppo/pilot/run.yml --mode train
PYTHONPATH=src .venv-wsl/bin/python ctrlp.py experiments/mirage/sla-v0/runs/shape_accuracy/ppo/pilot/run.yml --mode eval

Calibration selects one on-target constant intensity from 10 to 50 mW in 5 mW steps using validation mean IoU, then freezes it before testing. The laser is off at scan positions outside the target, as in the upstream constant-power baseline. Three baseline repetitions check numerical repeatability; they are not independent samples. PPO uses three seeds and 999424 transitions each, with eight training environments and the existing SB3 trainer. Only the final checkpoint is evaluated; no test-based checkpoint selection is performed.

runtime.eval.mode: environment runs the unchanged SB3 policy over the whole fixed bank in a batch, using the same JAX transitions as training. It requires unstacked, unnormalized inputs. Each case completes all 6400 transitions. Both arms write per-case IoU, missed target area, outside cure divided by target area, energy and return. Report random-instance and shape-family results separately and compare matched case IDs rather than reward alone.

Verification¶

Owner tests cover Gym, numerical safety, seeds, vector lifecycle and headless frames. Offline regression fixtures compare original and v4 physics, observations and reward against the pinned upstream module through step 6399, then check transition 6400’s reward without adopting upstream autoreset. Reference replay uses the CPU backend recorded in the fixture, keeping cross-device float32 rounding separate from port agreement. GPU execution is covered by the scalar/batch, lifecycle and baseline owner checks.

Refresh reference fixtures only after reviewing a pin change:

JAX_PLATFORMS=cpu .venv-wsl/bin/python tests/regression/fixtures/mirage_sla/generate.py /path/to/pinned/mirage-sla-rl

Generated traces, smoke checkpoints and successful checks are software evidence; they do not establish learned-policy benefit or physical print performance.
